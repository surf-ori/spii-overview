Way of working

Feedback on every deliverable is collected in the open, on GitHub, and every outcome stays visible.

Each of the six deliverables has its own repository and issue tracker. Anyone, a researcher, an institution, or an infrastructure provider, can open an issue against a deliverable: a correction, a gap, or a proposal. The issue template asks for your name, organisation, role, and which infrastructure you represent, so the origin of the feedback is clear alongside its content.

A curator reviews incoming issues for each deliverable. The curator checks feedback against that deliverable's scope, decides whether it is incorporated, and records the outcome directly on the issue (accepted, rejected, or already covered) with a short rationale before closing it. Nothing is handled silently: an issue's status and the reasoning behind it stay visible in the tracker, so the deliverables stay a community effort in practice, not only in name. Who curates each deliverable will be published here once assigned.

About SPII

SPII, the Strategic Plan for Integrated Open Science Infrastructure, develops a plan for a coherent and connected Open Science infrastructure that supports researchers and institutions in practicing Open Science. The current Dutch landscape is fragmented: many universities, universities of applied sciences and national service providers are independently building parts of an Open Science infrastructure. SPII's response is a reference model, a broadly supported framework that describes the functions and relationships an infrastructure needs, while leaving room for each component's own implementation choices.

Source: Towards a reference model for an open science infrastructure (SPII, version 20-07-2026).
